# Video Script: "Boondocking Without the Stress"

**Product:** Auto Generator Start (Onboard & Portable Generators)  
**Duration:** 50 seconds  
**Platforms:** Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok  
**Type:** Lifestyle + Aspirational  
**Target Audience:** Digital nomads, remote workers, boondocking enthusiasts

---

## Script

### [OPEN: Beautiful boondocking location, RV nestled in nature]

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "The best part of off-grid living? No power grid stress."

### [CUT TO: Interior shot - person working on laptop, coffee in hand]

**VOICEOVER:** "You're working remotely. Your A/C is running. Your fridge is cold."

### [CUT TO: Battery monitor showing slow voltage drop]

**VOICEOVER:** "Your batteries are slowly draining..."

### [CUT TO: Auto Generator Start system, light indicator comes on]

**VOICEOVER:** "...but your Auto Generator Start is watching."

### [CUT TO: Generator starting, power surging in]

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Automatic. Silent. Reliable."

**VOICEOVER:** "You keep working. Your RV keeps running. Zero interruption."

### [CUT TO: Sunset time-lapse over parked RV]

**VOICEOVER:** "That's the freedom of smart power management."

### [TEXT OVERLAY: "Auto Generator Start"]

### [TEXT OVERLAY: "For Onboard & Portable Generators"]

### [FINAL FRAME: FJ Solar Products logo]

### CTA: "Shop Now at FJ Solar Products"

## Key Messaging

- **Aspiration:** Freedom to work remotely from beautiful locations
- **Problem:** Power management anxiety while working
- **Solution:** Automatic, invisible power management
- **Benefit:** Zero interruption, peace of mind, freedom
- **Emotion:** Calm, peaceful, professional, independent

---

## Production Notes

- **Music:** Calm, ambient, inspirational (acoustic/minimal instrumentation)
- **Voiceover:** Warm, confident, slightly aspirational tone
- **Color Grade:** Golden hour, warm tones, professional editing
- **Pacing:** Slow, meditative throughout (no quick cuts)
- **Text Style:** Clean, minimal, elegant sans-serif
- **Sound Design:** Subtle keyboard typing, nature ambience, soft generator start (not jarring)
- **Vibe:** "Digital nomad lifestyle" aspirational content
